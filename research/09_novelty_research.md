# 09 · Novelty & AI-depth research: making Praman Setu read as an evidence engine
Date: 29-09-2026. Research was capped at about 8 minutes (6 web searches, no deep fetches). Items marked **[U]** are unverified: they come from background knowledge and were not re-checked today. Verify them before putting them on a slide.

**The risk we're fixing:** a judge sees "family name-matching for SC/ST certificates" and asks "is it just rules, and where's the AI?" (critique_3_jury.md). Other teams will probably pitch chatbots, WhatsApp bots, voice bots and dashboards.

**The move:** reposition Praman Setu from a caste-certificate matcher to **the evidence engine for Sewa Setu**. It reads documents, links people into families, learns from officers, explains itself and works across services. The officer still decides. Every addition below keeps these guardrails: no auto-reject; no caste inference; "no record found" is never a ground for rejection; data stays in the State Data Centre (DPDP purpose limitation).

## C. What NOT to add
- **A citizen chatbot / WhatsApp bot.** Other teams will have these, and Sewa Setu already has a WhatsApp channel (critique_3).
- **Any model that predicts "genuine vs fake caste claim"** or scores applicants. This is caste inference by the back door and legally toxic.
- **Auto-approval**, even for high-confidence matches. Keep "officer decides". Pre-verification is fine; issuing a certificate is not.
- **Face matching between old and new photos.** It is high-risk, biometric under DPDP, and weak on old photos.

## D. Q&A lines these additions unlock
- "Where's the AI?" → "Four learned components: a document reader, a probabilistic linkage model, active learning from officers, and a bounded agent. Plus a deterministic verifier around anything generative."
- "Only caste?" → Switch the dropdown to Domicile/Income live (#4, #6).
- "Why no LLM in orders?" → "Orders are legal text; LLMs only rewrite citizen explanations, and a verifier checks every fact." (#5)
