# Praman Sahayak: Improvement Loop Log
> **Renamed on 27-09-2026:** Praman Sahayak → **Praman Setu (प्रमाण सेतु)**. The round files below keep the old name as a historical record.
The goal of every round is **impact for the officer** (SDO Revenue for permanent caste certificates; Tehsildar for domicile). Their time budget is about 2 minutes per file, they carry the legal liability, and they are the ones who will adopt it or not.
Each round runs: critique (in-character officer + UX, tested live in the browser) → implement → test (browser + `npm run build` + `uv run pytest`).
Round files are in `app/iterations/roundN_critique.md` and `roundN_changes.md`.
